## The review

| # | Finding | Severity | Triage |
|---|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | In a `SignInWithout`, a device presented by someone other than its owner: their success cleared the owner's counters; under a policy that doesn't trust devices the owner's user counter was charged; the attempt passes the address's wait on the owner's pair counter | High | **Accepted in part.** A success clears counters only when they are the counters of the user the proof named; otherwise it takes back its count. A device the policy doesn't trust stands for no one: the attempt counts toward the address alone. Declined: passing the address's wait, since the attempt counts on the owner's pair counter, which waits after `throttle.free` like any other, and §5.8.6 already lets a device's holder pass the address's wait |
| 2 | A method told no user (before a sessionless flow's first proof) that names no user was accepted as the flow's login: a footgun for modules, and a known login answering apart from an unknown one | High | **Accepted.** Such a proof is refused (`user_mismatch`) |
| 3 | With no user's counter before the proof, a guessable module served through `SignInWithout` could be guessed from many addresses | Medium | **Accepted as documentation.** A22 says a method served through `SignInWithout` must have nothing to guess (a signature over the server's challenge, or an external assertion), as passkeys and OIDC; the engine can't tell |
| 4 | The tenth `Challenge` couldn't be proved: the cap hid the method as soon as it was issued | Medium | **Accepted.** A method stays offered past ten `Challenge`s while its last state is unused; five refusals still end it at once |
| 5 | Merging two proofs of one method takes the later time and keeps `Verified` if either had it | Medium | **Declined.** Nothing reads a proof's time together with its bit: `twoFactors` reads the bit, `Within` and §5.1.9 the time and the method. The merged record grants nothing two separate ones wouldn't. The check agreed |
| 6 | `anyDevice` trusted the token index without comparing the device's token hash | Low | **Accepted.** It compares them |
| 7 | A label the method gives skipped the cleaning and the cut `FactorLabel`'s gets | Low | **Accepted.** Both are cleaned and cut at 64 runes |
| 8 | An `Enroll` result without a key, or one over 1023 bytes, is the method's own failure: refunded, and with the engine's text | Low | **Declined.** `auth/passkey` refuses malformed keys itself (M7.2); the engine's check is a safety net, and its text reaches logs only (the error isn't public) |
| 9 | Racing `Challenge` against `Prove` makes `Verify` run for nothing | Low | **Declined.** Inherent in keeping `Verify` outside the write lock (§5.5.6); each state is still judged once |
| 10 | `Challenge`'s transaction doesn't check the offers again | Low | **Declined.** `Prove` checks them; a stale `Challenge` spends one of the flow's ten |

The reviewer found these hold up: credentials checked again in the write
transaction, nothing handed to a method before the first proof, one
judgement per state, two factors only from a `Verified` proof or two
proofs, no fallback to address codes when a module's factor is enrolled,
duplicate and limit checks inside the transaction, address-only
throttling of passkeys, no secret in events or errors, and `New`'s checks.
