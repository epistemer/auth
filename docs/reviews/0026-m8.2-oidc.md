# Review 0026: M8.2, the OIDC module

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-11-17 |
| Under review | The new module `auth/oidc` (`oidc.go`, `keys.go`, the tests with a provider of their own) and the CI change, with the locked contract (§4.12, §5.14, D37–D45, A26) |
| Reviewer | Gemini 4 Hydrogen (XHigh) through agy, about 281k tokens (Argon is out of quota). The review ran in a scratch folder holding only the diff and the excerpts |
| Triage | Claude |

Eight findings: six fixed, two fixed in part.

| # | Finding | Severity | Triage |
|---|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | go-oidc formats a key set's error with `%v`, so a key-set outage was answered as a bad token, counting against callers | Major | **Fixed.** Each `Verify` wraps the key set and keeps the outage itself; the answer is the method's own failure (`TestKeySet`) |
| 2 | A future `auth_time` passed the two-factor freshness check | Major | **Fixed**: refused past a minute ahead |
| 3 | Locks held across discovery and key fetches: callers piled up, one caller's cancellation stopped the fetch for all, a failing key set was refetched on every token | Major | **Fixed.** No lock across the network; concurrent callers share one fetch, which no caller's cancellation stops, and waiters leave when their own context ends; a fetch is tried at most once a minute whatever its outcome |
| 4 | A rotated key under the same `kid`, or with none, was never fetched | Major | **Fixed**: when no held key verifies a token, the set is fetched once (subject to the minute) and tried again |
| 5 | URLs with no host name or a spaced one passed; endpoints with a fragment; no block on private addresses | Major | **Fixed in part.** Host names and fragments are checked. Blocking private and loopback addresses was declined: company providers live on private networks, and the endpoints come from the configured issuer's document over TLS |
| 6 | Rules of §5.14 without a test that fails without them | Major | **Fixed in part.** Added: a key-set outage, rotation, a failed fetch keeping the keys, RSA 1024, P-384, `use: enc`, `alg: none`, HS256 with the public key, JSON serialization, no `id_token`, fractional dates, two audiences with our `azp`, a null `azp`, a future `auth_time`, an empty subject, long and unverified emails, the check order with two defects at once, a public client completing without a secret, an outage making no address wait, and `New`'s new refusals. Identity lifecycle and two-factor tests are the core's (M8.1) |
| 7 | Fractional `iat` and `auth_time` refused; `"azp": null` passed; a verified flag with no email | Minor | **Fixed** |
| 8 | JWS JSON serialization accepted; no `Valid()` or nil guard on keys; `key_ops` ignored | Minor | **Fixed in part**: compact only, keys checked with `Valid()` and for nil. go-jose doesn't parse `key_ops`, so `use` and `alg` alone filter |

The reviewer found these hold up: the algorithm and key filtering, PKCE,
`state` and `nonce` in constant time, RFC 9207, the callback order, the
client's redirects, timeout and response cap, secrets kept out of errors,
events and the proof's printing, and identities keyed by issuer and
subject, never found by email.

With the module's own 44 mutations and these fixes' 14, every mutation is
caught; those that survived at first were checks already made elsewhere,
and were removed.
